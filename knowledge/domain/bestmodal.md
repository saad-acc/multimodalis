# Definition
BestModal is the live product branded on the sandbox login screen as “BestModal / TARIFF ADMINISTRATION.” This repository is intended to hold a **second version** of that existing PHP application, not a new product name. The live sandbox is at `https://sandbox.bestmodal.de`. The legal footer on the login page names Acceltra GmbH (© 2026).
